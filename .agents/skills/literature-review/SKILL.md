# Literature Review

## Trigger
Use for theoretical foundations, literature searches, novelty claims, reference lists and paper/proposal writing.

## Procedure
1. State the precise research question, Game 1 mechanism and scope of review.
2. Search scholarly sources (publisher, journal, DOI/Crossref, institutional repository, Google Scholar as discovery only). Verify authors, year, title, venue, DOI and version using primary metadata where possible.
3. Organize a claim–source matrix: **concept; precise proposition; reference; evidence/location; model relevance; differences; limitations; confidence**.
4. Separate foundational production theory (Cobb-Douglas, fixed proportions, perfect substitutes, CES), bounded rationality/adaptive search, organizational learning, agent-based computational economics, experimental design and reproducibility.
5. Read beyond abstract where possible; do not imply a paper directly validates a specific implementation (e.g., probability .20 or search radius .10) unless it actually does.
6. Identify research gaps only after comparing what prior studies actually test. Distinguish novelty of question, mechanism, identification and implementation.
7. Produce accurate full references and DOI/URLs. Mark unverified bibliographic fields and inaccessible full texts; never fabricate pages, findings, quotations or citations.

## Required output
- Search strategy and date.
- Evidence table with source-level traceability.
- Critical synthesis including conflicting evidence.
- Explicit mapping of each model assumption to **literature-backed / researcher design choice / unverified**.
- References ready for Zotero, with verified metadata.

## Completion
Every literature-derived assertion has a checked source; no uncited claim of novelty or unsupported parameter justification.
