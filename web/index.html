Data provenance and limitations
This comparison describes one firm in one seed, rather than a 100-seed average or confidence interval. Game 1 does not simulate knowledge sharing, market competition, or AI investment. Each horizon uses its own original verified artifact. No shorter-horizon dataset is synthesized by truncating the 200-round artifact.
